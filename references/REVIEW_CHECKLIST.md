# Optional visual review checklist

Use this as a reminder after generation. It is not a scorecard, schema, or
required report. Inspect the actual image and use judgment.

## First look

- Exactly one character, with a coherent silhouette and one dominant idea.
- Complete body, hands, fingers, feet, both soles, extensions, and props inside
  the frame.
- Bright high-key rendering on a visually white neutral background.
- No text, logo, watermark, scenery, floor line, or extra character.

## LCD

- Exactly one horizontal LCD, close to front-facing and unobstructed.
- LCD opening and frame follow the head's depth, seams, materials, and
  perspective; nothing looks pasted on, floating, detached, or visibly shifted.
- Deep-black glass has a visible but restrained cobalt-blue inner gradient
  glow. The glow is not missing and does not become a large outside halo.
- Exactly two identical white square eyes, equal in size, level, and evenly
  spaced.
- Exactly one tall narrow black pupil inside each eye.
- Both pupils have the same slight RIGHT offset. Neither is centered; the pair
  is not mirrored.
- No mouth, nose, brows, lashes, cheeks, glasses, extra eyes, icons, letters,
  symbols, or other facial marks.

## Design and Level

- The requested motif, palette, materials, costume, prop, and mood are
  recognizable without becoming an accessory collage.
- Level 4 is built from consistently coarse square 3D modules rather than a
  pixelation filter.
- Level 5 has a strong large silhouette, precise sculpting, differentiated
  materials, and controlled fine detail rather than generic armor or a generic
  robot.
- In reference mode, the source's design language remains recognizable while
  its original face, marks, text, logos, background, and artifacts are absent.

## File and approval

- Mechanical checker confirms native 1024×1024 RGB PNG.
- A system QA pass is not human approval.
- The image remains outside `production/` until a human explicitly approves
  that exact candidate.
